# Rules for the leads folder

These rules apply to files in `leads/` only.

- Every person in this folder is made up. They are practice leads, not real people.
- Never add real people or real email addresses.
- Every row needs a `consent` value. Do not leave it blank.
- Never change the `@example.com` addresses. That domain can never receive mail, so no real person is ever emailed.

## Groups

- `weekend-bakers`: people who bake at home on weekends and want ideas, tips and fresh ingredients.
- `coffee-lovers`: people who come mainly for the coffee and enjoy a pastry on the side.
