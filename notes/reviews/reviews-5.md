# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: Reviews 81-100

Practice data. All reviewers and reviews are made up.

**81. Greta O. — 5 stars**
Lemon Verbena was bright and calm. I made a pot for a book club and everyone asked about it.

**82. Nathaniel W. — 2 stars**
Box arrived with a torn corner and one sachet missing. I wrote to customer service and got an automated reply only.

**83. Sasha P. — 4 stars**
Genmaicha was toasty with a hint of sea air. Great with sushi. I wish the sampler had more of it.

**84. Gus L. — 3 stars**
Assam was solid. Earl Grey was thin. Shipping took 9 days. Nothing terrible, nothing amazing.

**85. Lakshmi R. — 5 stars**
Masala Chai was perfect. I simmered it for five minutes in milk. My whole family had a cup.

**86. Brendan J. — 1 star**
Paid extra for fast delivery. It came after 14 days. No refund for the shipping fee. Not happy.

**87. Alice H. — 4 stars**
Jasmine Pearls were gorgeous. Slow to open but worth it. The leaflet gave a good tip on water temperature.

**88. Kenji M. — 5 stars**
Sencha was fresh and sweet. I used cooler water as the card suggested and it came out smooth.

**89. Verity S. — 3 stars**
Peppermint was bright, Chamomile was dull. The box was cute but felt flimsy.

**90. Obinna C. — 4 stars**
Yunnan Gold was rich and smooth. I made it with a little milk. Only one sachet, though. Wanted more.

**91. Harriet D. — 5 stars**
The Rooibos Vanilla was dessert in a cup. I had it by the fire with a book. A perfect gift to myself.

**92. Zachary B. — 2 stars**
Instructions were vague. "Steep to taste" is no help to a beginner. I made the Matcha like soup.

**93. Priya N. — 4 stars**
Darjeeling First Flush was delicate. I drank it plain in the afternoon. Delivery was reasonable at 6 days.

**94. Edmund F. — 3 stars**
Lapsang Souchong was a bold choice for a sampler. I did not love it, but I liked trying it.

**95. Constance A. — 5 stars**
The Oolong unfurled slowly and tasted of orchids and cream. The whole box felt carefully chosen.

**96. Theo K. — 4 stars**
Hibiscus Berry was tangy and bright. Made a big jug for a barbecue. Wish the sachet had been larger.

**97. Ruth G. — 1 star**
Two teas were out of date. I checked the sachets and the date was last year. Not acceptable.

**98. Dalia E. — 5 stars**
Moroccan Mint was my pick. I poured it from a height into little glasses, as the card suggested. Fun and tasty.

**99. Silas T. — 3 stars**
Gunpowder Green was too bitter. Ginger Lemon was nice. I wish the guide said how long to leave it.

**100. Willa Q. — 4 stars**
White Peony was gentle and sweet. I will try a full bag next. Delivery took nine days.
