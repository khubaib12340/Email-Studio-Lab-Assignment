# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: Reviews 101-120

Practice data. All reviewers and reviews are made up.

**101. Annika B. — 5 stars**
Earl Grey Cream was my favourite. Rich bergamot and a vanilla finish. The box was beautifully wrapped.

**102. Raymond S. — 2 stars**
Delivery took 17 days. The Assam was nice, but I needed it for a party. Too late to use as a gift.

**103. Giulia P. — 4 stars**
Jasmine Pearls were lovely. I wish there had been two sachets so I could share them.

**104. Emeka D. — 3 stars**
Teas were fine. The leaflet was confusing, with the steeping times in a table I could not follow.

**105. Hester M. — 5 stars**
Chamomile was soft and honeyed. Rooibos Vanilla was sweet and cosy. I have already bought a second box for my sister.

**106. Louis R. — 1 star**
Box arrived crushed. Three sachets burst open. I sent a photo and heard nothing back.

**107. Tabitha N. — 4 stars**
Masala Chai was full of spice and went well with milk. I wish the sampler had given me a second cup's worth.

**108. Ephraim K. — 5 stars**
The Darjeeling First Flush was bright and floral. I drank it in the morning with toast. Beautiful.

**109. Marguerite L. — 3 stars**
Matcha was grassy. I did not know how to prepare it. The leaflet said "whisk well" and nothing more.

**110. Dwayne F. — 4 stars**
Sencha and Genmaicha were both great. Packaging was sturdy. Shipping took a week.

**111. Cleo W. — 5 stars**
Lemon Verbena was the best surprise. Light and citrusy. I will buy a full bag.

**112. Alistair C. — 2 stars**
Peppermint tasted weak. Gunpowder Green was bitter. Only the Oolong was worth drinking. Expected more for the price.

**113. Noura H. — 4 stars**
Hibiscus Berry was zesty and bright. Nice over ice. The brewing leaflet was clear for once.

**114. Joss A. — 5 stars**
Yunnan Gold was a delight. Dark, malty and smooth. I wish I had ten sachets of it.

**115. Philomena G. — 3 stars**
Ginger Lemon was good, Moroccan Mint was too sweet. Box was pretty but too big for what was inside.

**116. Rohan V. — 4 stars**
White Peony was delicate and sweet. I brewed it twice and both cups were lovely. Delivery was fine.

**117. Eleanor J. — 5 stars**
I bought this for myself and ended up buying three more as gifts. The cards about each tea were a lovely touch.

**118. Bartholomew T. — 1 star**
Instructions said to use boiling water for everything. My green teas were ruined. Please fix the guide.

**119. Sofia O. — 4 stars**
Tie Guan Yin was creamy and floral. Rooibos Vanilla was a treat. I just wish each tea came in a bigger portion.

**120. Kit E. — 5 stars**
Lovely box from start to finish. Lapsang Souchong was a surprise, smoky and deep. I am glad I tried it.
