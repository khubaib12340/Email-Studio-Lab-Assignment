# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: Reviews 1-20

Practice data. All reviewers and reviews are made up.

**1. Marianne T. — 5 stars**
The Darjeeling First Flush was gorgeous. Light, floral, a bit like muscatel grapes. I have already ordered a full tin.

**2. Dev P. — 2 stars**
Box arrived 12 days after I ordered. The Assam was fine but the wait was too long for a gift.

**3. Colleen R. — 4 stars**
Loved the Jasmine Pearls. They unfurl in the cup and smell lovely. Only wish the sachets were bigger.

**4. Ibrahim S. — 3 stars**
Good teas, but the instructions said "steep until ready". Ready when? I over-brewed the Sencha and it went bitter.

**5. Fiona K. — 5 stars**
The Earl Grey Cream is the best I have had. Proper bergamot, smooth finish. Packaging was pretty too.

**6. Gareth W. — 1 star**
The box was crushed at one corner and two sachets had split. Tea dust all over the other teas. Disappointing.

**7. Noor A. — 4 stars**
Masala Chai was spicy and warming with a splash of milk. I would have liked three sachets, not one.

**8. Beth L. — 5 stars**
Lovely mix. The Rooibos Vanilla tasted like a pudding in a cup. My daughter stole half of it.

**9. Tomasz B. — 3 stars**
Lapsang Souchong is not for me. Far too smoky. The rest were nice, though, and the Gunpowder Green was crisp.

**10. Yuki H. — 4 stars**
The Genmaicha was toasty and savoury. Great with biscuits. Shipping took a week, which was okay.

**11. Priscilla O. — 2 stars**
Only one sachet of each tea. I found a favourite (the Oolong) and it was gone after one cup.

**12. Harvey D. — 5 stars**
Nice card with each tea saying where it came from. The Yunnan Gold was rich and malty. Great start to my mornings.

**13. Anika J. — 4 stars**
The Moroccan Mint was fresh and sweet. I used a glass teapot and it looked great.

**14. Stan M. — 3 stars**
Teas were fine. Nothing wrong, nothing special. Peppermint tasted a little flat to me.

**15. Rosalind C. — 5 stars**
Bought this as a treat for myself. The White Peony was delicate and soft. I sat and drank it slowly in the garden.

**16. Kofi N. — 1 star**
Ordered on the 3rd, arrived on the 19th. No update from anyone. The tea might be good but I will never know the freshness now.

**17. Lucia V. — 4 stars**
Hibiscus Berry was tart and ruby red. Lovely iced. The brewing times on the leaflet were a bit small to read.

**18. Dominic F. — 5 stars**
Chamomile was mellow and honeyed. The Matcha sachet was a nice surprise. Whisked it up and it was smooth.

**19. Gwen P. — 2 stars**
Brewing guide listed temperatures in Fahrenheit only. I guessed and ruined the Sencha. Please add Celsius.

**20. Rafael E. — 4 stars**
Ginger Lemon had a real kick. The box is sturdy enough to keep. Would buy again if there were more sachets.
