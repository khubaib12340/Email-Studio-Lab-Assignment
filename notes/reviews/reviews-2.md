# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: Reviews 21-40

Practice data. All reviewers and reviews are made up.

**21. Imogen S. — 5 stars**
The Lemon Verbena was bright and clean. I steeped it for four minutes and it was perfect. Lovely gift box.

**22. Patrick Q. — 2 stars**
Two of the eight teas were stale. The Peppermint smelled of cardboard. Not what I expected for the price.

**23. Sunita G. — 4 stars**
Assam was strong and malty, just what I like with milk. I wish the sampler had two sachets of it.

**24. Callum B. — 3 stars**
Fine selection. The box was larger than it needed to be, with lots of empty space and paper filler.

**25. Elena M. — 5 stars**
The Tie Guan Yin Oolong was floral and creamy. I got three infusions from one sachet. Wonderful.

**26. Jasper L. — 1 star**
Parcel left on a wet doorstep and the box was soaked. The courier is probably to blame, but the packaging was thin.

**27. Mei-Ling Z. — 4 stars**
Jasmine Pearls again a winner. The leaflet had a good tip about water temperature for green teas.

**28. Arthur H. — 3 stars**
Earl Grey was good but not as strong as I like. The Darjeeling was lovely though. Delivery was quick.

**29. Bridget N. — 5 stars**
I gave this to my mum for her birthday. She loved the Rooibos Vanilla and the pretty tin it came in.

**30. Oluwaseun A. — 2 stars**
Instructions were confusing. Some teas said "3 min" on the sachet and "5 min" on the leaflet. Which one is right?

**31. Hollie C. — 4 stars**
The Masala Chai was the star. Lots of cardamom and cinnamon. Simmered it in milk on the hob.

**32. Viktor R. — 5 stars**
Gunpowder Green was crisp and slightly smoky. Perfect after lunch. The whole box felt thoughtful.

**33. Janet W. — 3 stars**
Matcha was grassy and bitter for me. Maybe I whisked it badly. The leaflet did not explain how.

**34. Ashok D. — 4 stars**
Genmaicha was nutty and comforting. Wish there were more of it. I finished the sachet in two days.

**35. Poppy T. — 5 stars**
Hibiscus Berry made a gorgeous cold brew. My friends all asked where I got it.

**36. Leonard F. — 2 stars**
Shipping cost almost as much as the box. And it took 10 days. The tea was okay but not worth the hassle.

**37. Camille B. — 4 stars**
Chamomile was soft and sweet. Nice before bed. The packaging is recyclable, which I appreciated.

**38. Isaac O. — 5 stars**
Yunnan Gold was my favourite. Peppery, malty, a touch of cocoa. Would love a bigger bag.

**39. Margot E. — 3 stars**
Lapsang Souchong smelled like a campfire. Interesting, but too much. The Moroccan Mint helped me recover.

**40. Sanjay V. — 4 stars**
Sencha was fresh and grassy. I wish the instructions said to cool the water first. Mine came out harsh the first time.
