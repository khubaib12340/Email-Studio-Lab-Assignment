# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: Reviews 61-80

Practice data. All reviewers and reviews are made up.

**61. Odette V. — 5 stars**
The Tie Guan Yin was silky and floral. A real treat. I kept the box for my sewing buttons.

**62. Malcolm S. — 2 stars**
Teabags would have been easier. The loose leaf needs an infuser and none was included. Please mention that.

**63. Yasmin B. — 4 stars**
Rooibos Vanilla was creamy and sweet with no milk needed. Perfect with a biscuit. Wish I had more.

**64. Trevor L. — 3 stars**
Earl Grey was fine. I have had stronger ones. Box arrived on time and the packaging was neat.

**65. Imani J. — 5 stars**
Jasmine Pearls gave me three cups from one sachet. Delicate and fragrant. Wonderful gift for my aunt.

**66. Stefan H. — 1 star**
Instructions were in tiny grey print. I could not read them. Over-brewed everything. Bitter, all of it.

**67. Carys M. — 4 stars**
Darjeeling First Flush was crisp with a hint of apricot. I added no sugar. Delivery took 8 days.

**68. Anselm P. — 5 stars**
Yunnan Gold again my favourite. I sipped it black with a slice of toast. Box is going on my shelf.

**69. Winnie C. — 3 stars**
Chamomile was weak. Matcha was good. The leaflet was cute but the temperatures were hard to follow.

**70. Jorge F. — 4 stars**
Masala Chai made my house smell of cloves and cinnamon. Next time I will buy a full tin.

**71. Tess A. — 2 stars**
Shipping label said 3-5 days. It took 15. I bought this for a friend's birthday and missed it.

**72. Ravi K. — 5 stars**
Gunpowder Green was bold and slightly nutty. I loved the little cards on tea origin. Nicely done.

**73. Maud G. — 4 stars**
Peppermint was bright and fresh. I made it after dinner. Would have liked a second sachet.

**74. Dermot N. — 3 stars**
Hibiscus Berry was too sour for my taste. Rest of the box was okay. Packaging was recyclable.

**75. Aaliyah D. — 5 stars**
The Oolong was the highlight. Smooth, buttery, with a sweet finish. The box was well packed.

**76. Cormac R. — 4 stars**
Ginger Lemon was punchy. Loved it iced. Only wish the brewing guide listed grams per cup.

**77. Beatrix E. — 1 star**
One sachet was clearly open and damp. Tea smelled musty. I did not drink any of it.

**78. Lionel Q. — 4 stars**
Moroccan Mint tasted like a fresh garden. My kids liked it with a spoonful of sugar. Nice gift box.

**79. Pauline B. — 5 stars**
White Peony was soft and sweet. I drank it slowly and enjoyed every sip. Would buy again.

**80. Hamid T. — 3 stars**
The teas were good. The leaflet had too many words and not enough clear steps. Took me a while to work out the Sencha.
