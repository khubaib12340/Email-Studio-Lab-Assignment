# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: Reviews 41-60

Practice data. All reviewers and reviews are made up.

**41. Daphne K. — 5 stars**
The White Peony was subtle and sweet. I did not expect to like a white tea this much. Great introduction to new teas.

**42. Mario G. — 1 star**
Three of the sachets were open when the box arrived. Loose leaves everywhere. I threw the whole box out.

**43. Esther J. — 4 stars**
Ginger Lemon was zingy and warming. Loved it on a cold day. Only one sachet, sadly.

**44. Wilf P. — 3 stars**
Nice teas, but no way to tell which sachet was which without opening the leaflet. Labels were tiny.

**45. Tamara S. — 5 stars**
Earl Grey Cream was dreamy. I made a London Fog with it and my husband begged for another.

**46. Kwame B. — 2 stars**
Box took three weeks to arrive and customer service did not answer my email. The Assam was good but I am cross.

**47. Linnea H. — 4 stars**
Jasmine Pearls made the whole kitchen smell wonderful. I steeped them twice and both cups were good.

**48. Barnaby C. — 5 stars**
Darjeeling First Flush was elegant. Great with a slice of lemon cake. The leaflet was clear and friendly.

**49. Reema Q. — 3 stars**
Peppermint was fresh but nothing special. Rooibos Vanilla was sweeter than I like. Packaging was neat though.

**50. Ned A. — 4 stars**
Masala Chai again a hit with milk and a spoonful of honey. Wish there had been more than one serving.

**51. Francesca L. — 5 stars**
The Oolong was my new favourite. Buttery and smooth. I have already ordered a 100g bag.

**52. Hugo M. — 2 stars**
I followed the brewing guide for Matcha and got lumps. The guide never said to sift it. Wasted a pricey sachet.

**53. Adaeze N. — 4 stars**
Hibiscus Berry was sharp, fruity and pretty. A good iced tea for summer. Box was sturdy.

**54. Rupert F. — 3 stars**
Mixed bag. Yunnan Gold was great, Gunpowder Green was harsh. I think my water was too hot, and the guide did not warn me.

**55. Saoirse D. — 5 stars**
Everything about it felt like a gift. Ribbon, card, tissue paper. The Chamomile was lovely with honey.

**56. Zane R. — 4 stars**
Moroccan Mint was bold and cooling. I made a big pot for a family lunch and it went quickly.

**57. Philippa T. — 1 star**
Not what the photo showed. Photo had ten teas, I got six. Nobody explained the difference.

**58. Cedric O. — 4 stars**
Sencha was bright and clean. Instructions should say how many grams per cup. I guessed and was close.

**59. Naledi K. — 5 stars**
The Lemon Verbena was a lovely surprise. Tasted like a summer garden. I will order again for sure.

**60. Gideon W. — 3 stars**
Genmaicha was good, the rest average. Delivery was fast, but the box had a dent.
