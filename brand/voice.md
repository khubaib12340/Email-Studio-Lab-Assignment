# Brand Voice Guide

We are Rise & Crumb, a small neighbourhood bakery. We sell bread, pastries and coffee. Our readers are home bakers and coffee fans who like a friendly note.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Warm.** Friendly, like a chat across the counter.
- **Cheerful.** Light and happy. One exclamation mark is fine. No more.
- **Simple.** Short sentences and plain words.

## 2. Words we use

- fresh
- fresh from the oven
- crumb
- golden
- come in and say hello

## 3. Words we avoid

- Hype about prices, and pushy phrases such as "act now" or "don't miss out".
- Slang that a new reader may not know.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- No health claims of any kind. Do not say a product is healthy, good for you, light, low-sugar or good for energy, weight or digestion.
- Say only what is in the product. Describe only what it contains.
- No discounts or dates that are not confirmed. If it is not confirmed, leave it out.

## 5. How we sign off

Fresh from the oven,
Rise & Crumb
